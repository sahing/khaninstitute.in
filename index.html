About Khan Institute

Committed to Elevating Youth & Community in South 24 Parganas

Founded with the mission to empower local students with practical, income-generating skills, Khan Institute is located in Dholahat. We believe quality vocational education should be accessible and affordable to all.

From foundational computer literacy and competitive exam coaching to self-sustaining artisan crafts like tailoring and beautician training, we nurture talent right at the grassroots.
